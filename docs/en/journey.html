From Makkah to leading AI

A road with two scholarships abroad and two returns, each one to a larger responsibility.

  1. 2003–2008Makkah

    The start: engineering and sales at once

    He studied computer engineering at Umm Al-Qura University while working as a sales specialist and financial accountant at a medical products distributor from 2004 to 2007, handling clients and orders, budgets and financial reports.

    In the summer of 2007 he interned at Saudi Airlines' main headquarters in Jeddah, supporting systems across the building, including the CEO's office.

    • B.Sc. Computer Engineering, Umm Al-Qura University
    • Al Bader Medical Group
  2. 2008–2009King Fahad Security College

    Hajj season: planning under pressure

    He earned a diploma in security science with a grade of 94 and worked at the college as a planning and logistics engineer, leading a team responsible for logistics and subsistence for about 200 staff during the Hajj season, planning traffic flow and crowd control, and coordinating several agencies to solve problems in real time.

    • Diploma in Security Science, grade 94
  3. 2009–2013Riyadh

    Connecting the Kingdom on one network

    As a network engineer and project manager in a government entity, he planned and delivered the network extensions that connected all regional branches to headquarters in Riyadh, led the development and operation of the primary data center serving operations across the Kingdom, and prepared tender specifications and evaluated proposals.

    • Primary data center
    • Tenders and evaluations
  4. 2013–2016Bridgeport, USAAbroad

    First time abroad

    Sponsored by his employer, he earned a Master of Engineering in Computer Engineering at the University of Bridgeport with honors (GPA 3.67/4) and was invited to join Upsilon Pi Epsilon.

    He researched networks and embedded digital systems with sensor and Arduino prototypes and published at IEEE conferences, including an intelligent system to run the traffic and pedestrian tunnels of Makkah and Madinah.

    • M.Eng. Computer Engineering, honors
    • Two IEEE conference papers
  5. 2016–2018RiyadhReturn

    Back to the operations room

    He came back as Director of the Network Operations Division, ran it with high reliability and earned the Excellence Award in 2017.

    He led RFPs from conception to award under government procurement regulations, and delivered telecom circuits with STC, Mobily, ITC and MyPhone, a digital visitor queuing system, and a visual trial system in partnership with the Ministry of Justice.

    • Excellence Award 2017
    • With the Ministry of Justice
  6. 2018–2023Melbourne, AustraliaAbroad

    Second time abroad: the Ph.D. question

    Sponsored again, he pursued a Ph.D.

    in Networks and Telecommunications Engineering at RMIT University, within the Next Generation Telecommunications and Networks Research Group supported by the Australian Government and Telstra. He studied the global internet slowdowns caused by BGP convergence delays, proposed an SDN-based solution called MultiState-BGP, and published five peer-reviewed papers. He also taught networking labs and received the Ideal Teacher (Rose) Award in 2019 with a 93.18% teaching score.

    • Ph.D., RMIT University
    • Five peer-reviewed papers
  7. 2023 – todayRiyadhReturn

    Building AI from the ground up

    He returned to establish and lead the Artificial Intelligence department of a government entity: strategy, KPIs and a roadmap, a data warehouse, and use cases for operations, cybersecurity and decision-making.

    From 2023 to 2025 he also led E-Services & Transactions at the same time.

    • Director of AI Management
    • Director of E-Services 2023–2025
  8. 2025–2026Riyadh · Singapore

    Recognition and trust

    He received the Best Technology Leader Award 2025, 2nd place across all sectors and regions, and the Consultant Professional Accreditation from the Saudi Council of Engineers.

    In 2026 he was nominated among government leaders for the international government process reengineering program in Singapore.

    • Best Technology Leader 2025
    • Saudi Council of Engineers
